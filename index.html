TACTICS

Player vs Player

Two players on the same device. Take turns drafting units and battling for control of the plaza.

How to play

Draft: pick a class for each unit and place them on your base tiles.

On your turn, select a unit, then click a highlighted tile to move. Use Attack or Skill, then End Turn.

Reach the center plaza to level up to 2, the enemy base to level 3. Control the plaza or eliminate the enemy to win.

Player vs CPU

Face the AI. You control your army; the CPU drafts and plays automatically. Great for practice or a quick match.

How to play

Same rules as PvP. Draft your lineup, then take your turns while the CPU responds. Use the same tactics—control the plaza and pick off enemy units.

CPU vs CPU

Watch two AI armies draft and fight. Useful for testing strategies or running many games in a row.

How to play

No input needed. Choose AI draft style and number of games (dev). The match runs automatically.

Story

A level progression mode with different maps and enemies. Fight through a campaign and unlock new challenges.

How to play

Complete levels in order. Each stage has a unique map and enemy lineup. Win to advance and take on harder battles.

Settings

No settings for this mode.
