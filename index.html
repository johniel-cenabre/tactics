How to play

Draft: pick a class for each unit and place them on your base tiles.

On your turn, select a unit, then click a highlighted tile to move.

Use Attack or Skill to target enemies in range, then End Turn.

Get to the center plaza to level up to 2.

Get to the enemy base to level up to 3.

Control the center plaza or eliminate the enemy to win.
